Common questions

What devices and languages does Still support?

Still requires iOS 18 or later. It follows the system language for English, Simplified Chinese, or Traditional Chinese, with English as the fallback.

Do I need a Still account?

No. Personal features use local app data. Family synchronization uses Apple iCloud accounts and a private CloudKit invitation. App Store purchases use the Apple Account for Media & Purchases.

How do I connect a child’s device?

Set up Apple Family Sharing with the appropriate parent/guardian and child accounts. On the child’s device, choose Child and complete Apple’s Screen Time authorization with the guardian. In the parent workspace, create a private invitation and invite the intended child’s Apple Account. Accept the system invitation on the child’s device and open Still on both devices.

Why does a family change say Waiting?

Waiting means the requested change was saved but the child has not yet returned the matching application receipt. Update Still to the latest version on both devices first. Check iCloud, network access, and Screen Time authorization, then open Still on both devices. Applied appears only after a matching child confirmation. Background delivery can be delayed, and force quitting the child app can prevent updates.

Is Hide the same as iOS “Hide and Require Face ID”?

Still’s Hide uses Apple’s Screen Time controls for 1–50 individually selected apps. Categories and websites use restrictions. Apple’s own Home Screen “Hide and Require Face ID” feature is separate and must be operated by the user in iOS.

What does a daily limit alert mean?

It records that Apple delivered a callback for a configured threshold, with the rule name, selected token, minutes, and callback time. It is not an exact usage total or ranking. A pause or hide action may also apply if configured. Alerts and cloud delivery can be delayed.

Why is an Apple activity report empty or different from my paired device?

Reports depend on Apple’s authorization, Family Sharing, available activity, and system-provided scope. The report’s people/device labels are not proof that the report belongs to the currently paired CloudKit child. Still does not replace missing reports with threshold-event estimates.

How do I end family management?

In the parent workspace, choose Turn off all family restrictions and wait for the child’s matching receipt. Then use End management and Apple’s sharing interface to stop sharing. A guardian should revoke Still’s Screen Time access in iOS Settings on the child’s device. Open Still on both devices. On the child, Check management recovery becomes usable when pairing has ended and authorization is revoked.

How can I export or delete my data?

Adult Settings → Personal data exports or deletes the personal workspace. Parent weekly reflections and local week-plan records have separate controls. These do not erase all family cloud history. Ending a share does not erase the owner’s cloud records or recipients’ copies. Review Still’s storage in iOS Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Storage / Manage Account Storage, using options Apple provides, or contact us privately for guidance. Delete exported files and backups separately where needed.

Apple: third-party app data in iCloud

Which features are free?

Screen Time controls, shielding/hiding, schedules, threshold alerts, basic remote management, release, appearance, export, and deletion are free. Pro adds independent planning, goals, agreements, tasks, and reflection features. Child mode has no purchase entry. Saved records remain available to read, complete, export, or delete when Pro ends.

How do I restore or cancel a purchase?

In an adult workspace, open Settings → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. Manage an active subscription in Settings → Manage subscription or Apple’s subscription settings. Deleting Still does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Who can see family notes and check-ins?

The owner and accepted participants in the private family share can see shared family content, including notes, submissions, feedback, and check-ins. Do not put information there that you do not want those participants to see. Personal goals/reflections and parent weekly reflections use local storage instead.
