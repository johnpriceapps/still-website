Room to begin

The essentials stay free.

Device controls are part of the free experience. Pro adds a separate set of planning tools.

Free

Your everyday controls

The tools for managing device access, with no Pro purchase required.

  • Screen Time controls, hiding, and shielding
  • Schedules and threshold reminders
  • Family remote management and release
  • Appearance, personal data export, and deletion
Optional Pro

A little more direction

Independent tools for the things you want to make time for.

  • Personal plans and goals
  • Family plans and agreements
  • Tasks and small next steps
  • Check-ins and reflections

Purchases are offered to adults only. Children have no purchase entry. Apple handles any in-app purchase; current terms and prices appear in the app.

Your family.
Your space.

Still has no separate account to create and no developer-operated server. Family sharing uses Apple’s private CloudKit sharing.

Personal stays personal.
Personal goals and reflections stay on your device. You choose whether to use family sharing.
Share with intention.
Shared family records may include rules, plans, tasks, responses, and feedback. Still does not copy raw Screen Time reports or precise total usage into its family CloudKit share.
You keep the controls.
Use Settings to export or delete supported personal data stored on your device. Read the policy for family sharing, iCloud, and copies held by other people.
