Who is responsible

Still is provided by its developer, known on GitHub as johnpriceapps. In this policy, “we” means that developer. Use the private contact link on this page for privacy questions or requests.

Information on your device

Still has no separate Still account or developer-operated app server. Personal goals, tasks, reflections, session history, saved personal plans, and parent weekly reflections are stored on your device. Still does not sync those personal records through its family CloudKit share.

The app also stores preferences, your selected workspace, opaque Apple app selections, control settings, and local family caches or unsent edits needed to operate the features you choose. Device backups may include local app data depending on your Apple settings.

Information shared with your family

Family mode uses Apple iCloud and a private CloudKit share. Family records are available to the owner and the participants you invite and who accept the share. They are not posted to a public Still feed. Invite only the intended family member and review invited members in the app.

Shared information is used to synchronize controls, show delivery and response status, coordinate plans, and preserve family task history. It can include:

  • Family pairing identifiers, Apple-provided participant identifiers, record authors, revisions, and timestamps; Apple’s invitation interface may also display participant information.
  • Selected apps, categories, or websites represented by opaque Apple tokens; pause/hide settings, schedules, time zone, and device application receipts.
  • Daily threshold rules and rule names, configured minutes, and threshold-reached events with callback times. A reached event is not an exact total of use.
  • Agreements, tasks, dates, steps, notes, change requests and replies, submissions, and parent feedback.
  • Family week-plan setup, including the age you enter, available time, assistance limits, materials, activity choices, published plans, confirmations, adjustment requests, and check-ins.

Screen Time reports and authorization

Still uses Apple’s Screen Time frameworks and system authorization. Personal controls use individual authorization. Child controls require the guardian authorization provided by Apple for a child in Family Sharing; accepting an iCloud invitation alone does not establish guardianship.

When you open an authorized Apple activity report, the report extension processes and displays the usage information Apple makes available, such as today’s duration, recent daily totals, app usage, and person/device labels. This processing stays inside the report extension. Still does not copy raw reports, exact totals, app rankings, or extracted app identities into its family CloudKit records.

If enabled, Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode confirms sensitive actions through Apple’s system interface. Still receives the authentication result, not biometric templates or your passcode.

Apple services, purchases, and notifications

Apple provides iCloud storage and sharing, Screen Time authorization and reports, App Store purchases, and push delivery. These services handle information under Apple’s terms and privacy practices. Their processing and storage locations are governed by Apple, and may differ from your location.

Still checks verified StoreKit transactions and entitlement status on the device to enable Pro. Apple handles payment details; Still does not receive your payment card number or write purchase history into the family share. Child mode has no purchase entry.

Apple may also provide the developer with sales and subscription reports. If you enable sharing app analytics with developers in Apple’s settings, Apple may share usage statistics and diagnostic reports; TestFlight has its own feedback and diagnostic sharing rules. These Apple reports are separate from shared family records and do not require an analytics SDK in Still.

Optional parent notifications use Apple’s delivery services. The visible message is a general limit-reached alert; notification metadata can identify the relevant record or rule. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings. Delivery and background updates are not guaranteed to be immediate.

Your controls, export, and deletion

In an adult workspace, Settings → Personal data provides export and deletion for the personal workspace. Parent weekly reflections and local week-plan records have their own export/deletion controls. These actions apply to the records described by the control; deleting a local week plan does not delete its shared cloud version.

To end family management, the parent should turn off all family restrictions, wait for the child’s matching confirmation, then use End management and Apple’s sharing interface to stop sharing. A guardian should also revoke Still’s Screen Time access in iOS Settings on the child’s device. Keep both devices online and reopen Still so they can detect the change. Child workspace recovery checks both the pairing and authorization state.

Stopping sharing revokes future shared access; it is not a command to erase every family record, another device’s saved copy, exported file, screenshot, or backup. Shared records can remain in the owner’s iCloud storage. The current app does not provide a complete family-cloud export or bulk-delete button.

The iCloud owner can review Still’s storage in Apple Account → iCloud → Storage / Manage Account Storage in iOS Settings and use the deletion options Apple makes available. Options vary with iOS and account state. If Still is not listed or you need help with particular shared records, contact us privately for guidance. Do not assume uninstalling the app or disabling iCloud removes all cloud records.

Saved records have no fixed automatic deletion period in Still and can remain until you remove them using the applicable controls. Apple manages its own service retention and backups. Files you export or share are controlled by you and their recipients. Export and deletion controls remain free after Pro ends.

Children and family roles

Family features are intended to be set up and supervised by a parent or guardian. Discuss with the child what will be shared, use only necessary information, and avoid entering health details, school identifiers, addresses, or other sensitive information into notes.

A child’s task responses and check-ins are shared family content, not private messages to the developer. Apple’s authorization is a separate system safeguard; it is not a substitute for any parental permission required by applicable law. A parent or guardian can use the private contact link for questions or requests about a child’s information.

Advertising, analytics, and security

Still does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, sell personal information, or link app data with other companies’ data for targeted advertising. The bundled Lottie library renders local onboarding animations; Still does not use it to send family content to an animation service.

The app relies on Apple’s device protections, permissions, and private CloudKit sharing. Authorized family participants can access shared records. No security measure can guarantee that a recipient will not retain or copy information; choose family invitees carefully.

This website and support

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising cookies, third-party fonts, or a form that uploads your app records. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security when serving Pages sites, whether or not the visitor is signed in. GitHub’s privacy statement explains its handling of service and request information.

If you contact support, we receive the contact details and information you choose to send. We use them to answer the request, investigate the reported issue, or handle a privacy request, and keep them while needed for those purposes and any applicable legal requirements. Send only necessary details; do not send passwords, purchase credentials, raw activity reports, or a child’s private records.

GitHub issues are public and are only for general, non-sensitive support. Use the private contact link for privacy requests and children’s information. Email and GitHub are separate services with their own privacy practices; GitHub’s processing can involve other countries.

Privacy questions and requests
